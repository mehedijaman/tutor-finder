## Introduction

Welcome to **Tutor Finder** – the smart marketplace connecting tutors and families!

Tutor Finder is a modern platform that helps:
- **Guardians** find qualified tutors for their children
- **Tutors** discover job opportunities and build their careers
- **Administrators** manage the platform efficiently

This manual will guide you through all features of the platform.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the Tutor Finder website
2. Click **"Register"** in the top navigation
3. Choose your role:
   - **I'm a Guardian** – Looking for tutors
   - **I'm a Tutor** – Looking for tutoring jobs
4. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Password
5. Verify your phone number via SMS OTP
6. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox
7. Complete your profile

### Logging In

1. Click **"Login"** in the top navigation
2. Enter your email and password
3. If you have Two-Factor Authentication enabled, enter your code
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Password Recovery

1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your email for the reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password

## For Guardians

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see your Guardian Dashboard with:
- **Active Jobs** – Jobs you've posted that are still open
- **Applications** – Tutors who have applied to your jobs
- **Hired Tutors** – Currently assigned tutors
- **Recent Activity** – Latest updates on your jobs

### Posting a Tuition Job

1. Click **"Post New Job"** from your dashboard
2. Fill in the job details:

   **Basic Information**
   - Job Title (e.g., "Math Tutor Needed for Grade 8")
   - Subject(s) – Select from the list
   - Class/Grade Level
   - Tuition Type (Home, Online, Group, etc.)

   **Schedule & Location**
   - Days per week
   - Preferred time slots
   - Location (Area, City)

   **Requirements**
   - Preferred tutor gender (Male/Female/Any)
   - Experience requirements
   - Additional requirements

   **Budget**
   - Monthly salary range
   - Fee payment mode (Monthly, Weekly, etc.)

3. Click **"Post Job"**
4. Your job will appear on the Job Board

### Managing Job Applications

1. Go to **"My Jobs"** from the menu
2. Click on a job to see applications
3. For each application, you can:
   - **View Profile** – See tutor's full profile
   - **Accept** – Hire this tutor for the job
   - **Reject** – Decline the application

### Hiring a Tutor

1. Review applications for your job
2. Click **"Accept Application"** for your chosen tutor
3. The tutor will be notified
4. Once confirmed, the job status changes to "Assigned"

### Managing Active Assignments

1. Go to **"My Assignments"** from the menu
2. View your hired tutors and their details
3. You can:
   - Contact the tutor
   - View assignment details
   - Request cancellation if needed

### Verification

To access all features, complete your profile verification:

1. Go to **"My Profile"** → **"Verification"**
2. Submit required documents:
   - Valid ID (National ID, Passport, etc.)
   - Profile photo
3. Pay the verification fee via bKash or SSLCommerz
4. Wait for admin approval (usually within 24-48 hours)

## For Tutors

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see your Tutor Dashboard with:
- **Available Jobs** – Jobs matching your profile
- **My Applications** – Jobs you've applied to
- **Active Assignments** – Current tutoring jobs
- **Earnings Summary** – Your financial overview

### Completing Your Profile

A complete profile increases your chances of getting hired:

1. Go to **"My Profile"** from the menu
2. Fill in all sections:

   **Basic Information**
   - Profile photo
   - Bio/Introduction
   - Date of birth
   - Gender

   **Education**
   - Add your educational qualifications
   - Include institution, degree, year, result

   **Teaching Experience**
   - Subjects you can teach
   - Grade levels
   - Years of experience
   - Tuition types (Home, Online, etc.)

   **Availability**
   - Available days
   - Preferred time slots
   - Preferred locations

   **Expected Salary**
   - Monthly salary expectation

3. Click **"Save Profile"**

### Account Verification

Verification builds trust with guardians:

1. Go to **"My Profile"** → **"Verification"**
2. Submit required documents:
   - Valid ID (National ID/Passport)
   - Educational certificates
   - Profile photo
3. Pay the verification fee
4. Wait for admin approval

### Browsing the Job Board

1. Go to **"Browse Jobs"** from the menu
2. Use filters to find relevant jobs:
   - Subject
   - Location (City, Area)
   - Class/Grade
   - Tuition Type
   - Salary Range
3. Click on a job to view details

### Applying for Jobs

1. Find a job you're interested in
2. Click **"Apply Now"**
3. Write a cover note (optional but recommended)
4. Click **"Submit Application"**
5. Track your application status in **"My Applications"**

### Application Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Guardian hasn't reviewed yet |
| Reviewed | Guardian has seen your application |
| Accepted | You got the job! |
| Rejected | Application not selected |

### Managing Assignments

1. Go to **"My Assignments"** from the menu
2. View your active tutoring jobs
3. For each assignment, you can see:
   - Guardian contact info
   - Schedule details
   - Payment status

### Notifications

Stay updated with notifications:

1. Click the bell icon in the top navigation
2. View all notifications
3. Click **"Mark as Read"** for individual notifications
4. Click **"Mark All as Read"** to clear all

## For Administrators

### Admin Dashboard

Access the admin panel at `/admin`:

1. Log in with admin credentials
2. View the dashboard with:
   - Total users, tutors, guardians
   - Active jobs count
   - Revenue statistics
   - Recent activities

### User Management

**View Users**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Users**
2. Filter by role (Tutor, Guardian, Admin)
3. Search by name, email, or phone
4. Click on a user to view details

**Manage User**
- **View Profile** – See complete user information
- **Edit** – Modify user details
- **Suspend** – Temporarily disable account
- **Delete** – Remove user (soft delete)
- **Impersonate** – Log in as user (for support)

### Job Management

**View Jobs**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Jobs**
2. Filter by status (Open, Assigned, Closed, Cancelled)
3. Search by title or location

**Manage Job**
- View job details and applications
- Close or cancel jobs
- View assignment history

### Verification Requests

**Process Verifications**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Verifications**
2. Filter by status (Pending, Approved, Rejected)
3. Click on a request to review

**Review Process**
1. View submitted documents
2. Check payment status
3. **Approve** – Verify the user
4. **Reject** – Deny verification with reason

### Finance Management

**Invoices**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Finance** → **Invoices**
2. View all invoices
3. Filter by status
4. Export reports

**Payments**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Finance** → **Payments**
2. View payment transactions
3. Track gateway responses

**Refund Requests**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Finance** → **Refunds**
2. Review refund requests
3. Approve or reject with reason
4. Process payouts

**Wallet Ledger**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Finance** → **Ledger**
2. View all ledger entries
3. Track journal entries for auditing

### Content Management

**Blog**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Blog**
2. Create, edit, or delete blog posts
3. Manage categories and tags

**FAQs**
1. Go to **Admin** → **FAQs**
2. Add/edit FAQs for Tutors and Guardians

**Testimonials**
1. Go to **Admin** → **Testimonials**
2. Manage customer testimonials

### Taxonomy Management

Manage platform taxonomies:
- Countries, Cities, Areas
- Categories, Subjects
- School Classes
- Tuition Types

1. Go to **Admin** → **Taxonomies**
2. Select the taxonomy type
3. Add, edit, or deactivate items

### System Settings

**Site Settings**
- Site name, logo, favicon
- Contact information
- Social media links
- SEO settings

**SMTP Settings**
- Configure email server
- Test email delivery

**SMS Settings**
- Configure SMS gateway
- Test SMS delivery

**Payment Gateways**
- Configure bKash credentials
- Configure SSLCommerz credentials
- Enable/disable gateways

## Account Settings

### Profile Settings

1. Click your name in the top navigation
2. Select **"Settings"**
3. Update your profile information
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Password Change

1. Go to **Settings** → **Password**
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter your new password
4. Confirm new password
5. Click **"Update Password"**

### Two-Factor Authentication

Enable extra security for your account:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Security**
2. Click **"Enable Two-Factor Authentication"**
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
4. Enter the 6-digit code to verify
5. Save your recovery codes securely

### Appearance Settings

1. Go to **Settings** → **Appearance**
2. Choose your preferred theme:
   - Light Mode
   - Dark Mode
   - System Default
3. Settings are saved automatically

### Delete Account

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**
2. Click **"Delete Account"**
3. Enter your password to confirm
4. Your account will be permanently deleted

⚠️ **Warning**: This action cannot be undone. All your data will be lost.

## Payments & Finance

### Payment Methods

Tutor Finder supports:
- **bKash** – Mobile financial service
- **SSLCommerz** – Credit/Debit cards and bank transfers

### Making a Payment

1. Access your invoice (e.g., verification fee)
2. Click **"Pay Now"**
3. Select your preferred payment method
4. Complete payment on the gateway
5. You'll be redirected back upon completion

### Viewing Invoices

**For Guardians/Tutors:**
1. Go to **Finance** → **Invoices**
2. View all your invoices
3. Filter by status (Paid, Unpaid, etc.)
4. Download invoice PDF

### Wallet Balance

**For Tutors:**
1. Go to **Finance** → **Wallet**
2. View your current balance
3. See transaction history
4. Request withdrawal

### Requesting a Refund

1. Go to **Finance** → **Request Refund**
2. Select the payment to refund
3. Enter refund reason
4. Submit request
5. Wait for admin approval

### Refund Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Awaiting admin review |
| Approved | Refund approved, processing |
| Paid | Refund completed |
| Rejected | Refund denied |

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is Tutor Finder free to use?**
A: Registration is free. Verification requires a small fee. No commission is charged on tutoring payments.

**Q: How do I verify my account?**
A: Go to your profile, submit required documents, pay the verification fee, and wait for admin approval.

**Q: Can I use the platform on mobile?**
A: Yes, Tutor Finder is fully responsive and works on all devices.

### For Guardians

**Q: How do I find a tutor?**
A: Post a job with your requirements, and qualified tutors will apply. You can also browse tutor profiles.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with a tutor?**
A: You can cancel the assignment and post a new job. Contact support for assistance.

**Q: Are tutors verified?**
A: Verified tutors have a badge on their profile. We verify identity and educational credentials.

### For Tutors

**Q: How do I increase my chances of getting hired?**
A: Complete your profile 100%, get verified, write compelling applications, and maintain good ratings.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payment arrangements are made directly with guardians. The platform does not handle tutor payments.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple jobs?**
A: Yes, you can apply for as many jobs as you want.

### Payments

**Q: What payment methods are supported?**
A: bKash (mobile) and SSLCommerz (cards/bank transfer).

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, all payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways. We don't store card details.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
A: Refunds are processed within 3-7 business days after approval.
